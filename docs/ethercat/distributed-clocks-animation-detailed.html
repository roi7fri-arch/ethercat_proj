EtherCAT Distributed Clocks — how it achieves sub-microsecond synchronization

A worked, animated walk-through of the mechanism (following the ETG / Beckhoff “Distributed Clocks” figures). The key idea: a slave does not act when the frame arrives — it acts when its own hardware clock reaches a pre-agreed time. So communication jitter (µs) never becomes actuation jitter (ns). Below we measure delays, align clocks, and fire SYNC0 together — with real numbers.
